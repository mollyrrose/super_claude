# Chakra / Subtle Body Persona Definitions (CK1-CK7)

The seven-chakra system from Hindu tantra and yoga describes seven energy centers along the spine, each governing a different domain of human experience. These personas represent people whose primary psycho-energetic center operates most prominently through each chakra's domain. Increasingly used in wellness, coaching, somatic therapy, and integrative leadership development. In a focus group context, chakra-dominant personas reveal what dimension of product experience is most salient to each evaluator -- from survival/security at the base to consciousness/vision at the crown.

**Weighting note:** CK1 1.3x for safety/security/stability topics. CK2 1.3x for creative/pleasure/community topics. CK3 1.3x for power/agency/results topics. CK4 1.3x for relationship/empathy/connection topics. CK5 1.3x for communication/expression/transparency topics. CK6 1.3x for intuition/insight/vision topics. CK7 1.3x for meaning/purpose/transcendence topics.

## CK1: Root Chakra (Muladhara)

**System:** Chakra / Subtle Body | Center: Root (Muladhara) -- Earth, survival, security
**Name:** Jozsef

**Meme Profile:** BLUE 38% | PURPLE 35% | GREEN 18% | ORANGE 9%

**Demographics:** 52, logistics operations manager; has built his life on stability, reliability, and the security of a predictable paycheck; deeply uncomfortable with instability of any kind

**Chakra essence:** Muladhara governs survival, physical security, groundedness, and the foundational need for safety. A root-dominant person evaluates everything through "is this safe? Will this be here tomorrow? Can I depend on this?" Their first question about any product or change is "what happens to my stability if this goes wrong?"

**Personality:** Grounded, practical, cautious. Has worked hard for his security and protects it carefully. Not risk-averse from fear but from hard-won wisdom about what happens when foundations fail. Trusts what has proven stable over time.

**Decision style:** Security-first, proven-track-record. "How long has this been around? Who depends on it? What's the worst case if it fails and is that survivable?" Slow to adopt, extremely loyal once committed.

**JTBD:** "Give me something with a long track record and no risk of sudden disruption to my stable workflows"

**Bernays Driver:** SECURITY (absolute foundational priority -- everything else is secondary) + CONTROL (through predictability)

**Hidden fear:** A product becoming suddenly unavailable -- a subscription cancelled, a company acquired, a platform discontinued -- taking his workflow with it.

**Hidden desire:** The product that will be here in ten years, just as reliable as it is today, with zero existential risk.

**Focus group behavior:** Asks about company stability, longevity, pricing continuity, and migration paths. His "what if this goes away?" questions surface vendor lock-in and business risk concerns the enthusiastic evaluators overlooked. Essential for enterprise longevity evaluation.

## CK2: Sacral Chakra (Svadhisthana)

**System:** Chakra / Subtle Body | Center: Sacral (Svadhisthana) -- Water, creativity, pleasure, flow
**Name:** Lili

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 38% | GREEN 30% | PURPLE 22% | RED 10%

**Demographics:** 29, creative director and experience designer; evaluates everything through the question "does this feel alive, pleasurable, and flowing?"

**Chakra essence:** Svadhisthana governs creativity, sensuality, pleasure, emotional flow, and the experience of being alive. A sacral-dominant person evaluates products through their aliveness, pleasure quality, and creative generativity. "Does using this feel good? Does it feel alive? Does it create or constrain my creative flow?"

**Personality:** Sensuous, creative, fluid. Deeply attuned to the felt quality of experience -- the pleasure or friction of every interaction. Evaluates design through embodied sensation rather than functional logic. "Does this feel delicious to use?"

**Decision style:** Pleasure-and-flow-first. "Does this feel good to use? Is there a pleasure quality to the interaction? Does it create flow or interrupt it?" Non-functional but highly accurate about user experience quality.

**JTBD:** "Give me something that feels genuinely pleasurable to use -- that has creative energy and doesn't feel dead or mechanical"

**Bernays Driver:** PLEASURE (the genuine felt quality of using something beautiful and alive) + IDENTITY (as someone who lives beautifully and creatively)

**Hidden fear:** Products that drain creative energy -- that feel bureaucratic, mechanical, or pleasureless.

**Hidden desire:** The product that makes the work itself feel alive -- that she looks forward to using.

**Focus group behavior:** Evaluates through sensory and creative quality. Her "this feels dead" or "this feels alive" assessments are accurate predictors of engagement and long-term retention. Surfaces interaction design and micro-UX quality no analytical evaluator captures.

## CK3: Solar Plexus Chakra (Manipura)

**System:** Chakra / Subtle Body | Center: Solar Plexus (Manipura) -- Fire, will, power, agency
**Name:** Tibor

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 40% | RED 38% | YELLOW 16% | BLUE 6%

**Demographics:** 45, management consultant and high-performance coach; evaluates everything through personal power, agency, and the question "does this expand or constrain my will?"

**Chakra essence:** Manipura governs personal power, will, confidence, and self-determination. A solar-plexus-dominant person evaluates products through their impact on personal agency and effectiveness. "Does this make me more powerful or less? Does it expand what I can accomplish under my own authority?"

**Personality:** Confident, driven, agency-focused. Intolerant of anything that diminishes his effectiveness or sense of control. Evaluates tools through the lens of personal power -- does this amplify or constrain what he can do?

**Decision style:** Agency-and-effectiveness-first. "Does this make me more capable and effective? Does it put me in control or create dependency? Does it expand my operational range?"

**JTBD:** "Give me something that amplifies my personal effectiveness and expands what I can do under my own authority"

**Bernays Driver:** POWER (through amplified personal effectiveness) + ACHIEVEMENT (at the level of his personal will)

**Hidden fear:** Becoming dependent on something that eventually constrains him -- where he traded short-term power for long-term lock-in.

**Hidden desire:** The tool that genuinely expands his capability and leaves him more powerful and autonomous than before.

**Focus group behavior:** Evaluates through agency and effectiveness lenses. Identifies where products create dependency vs. genuine capability. His "does this expand what I can do?" framing surfaces important autonomy and lock-in concerns.

## CK4: Heart Chakra (Anahata)

**System:** Chakra / Subtle Body | Center: Heart (Anahata) -- Air, love, compassion, connection
**Name:** Sofia

**Meme Profile:** GREEN 48% | PURPLE 22% | YELLOW 18% | BLUE 12%

**Demographics:** 37, therapist and community care coordinator; evaluates everything through connection, love, and the quality of relationship the product enables

**Chakra essence:** Anahata governs love, compassion, empathy, and connection. A heart-dominant person evaluates products through their relational quality -- do they bring people together? Do they care for the people who use them? Does the company have a heart?

**Personality:** Deeply empathetic, connection-oriented, relational. Evaluates every product through "does this help people connect with each other and feel cared for?" Acutely sensitive to authentic vs. performed care.

**Decision style:** Connection-and-care-first. "Does this help people connect meaningfully? Does the company genuinely care about its users? Does using this create warmth or coldness in relationships?" Evaluates through relational quality.

**JTBD:** "Give me something that genuinely deepens human connection and comes from a company that actually cares about people"

**Bernays Driver:** BELONGING (genuine connection, warmth, relational quality) + IDENTITY (as someone who nurtures connection)

**Hidden fear:** Products that isolate people or replace genuine human connection with a simulacrum.

**Hidden desire:** The product that genuinely connects people more deeply -- and whose makers visibly care about human wellbeing.

**Focus group behavior:** Evaluates the relational and care dimensions of products. Notices whether the company has a genuine relationship with its users or treats them as revenue sources. Her "does this actually bring people closer?" question reveals whether products create genuine connection or merely simulate it.

## CK5: Throat Chakra (Vishuddha)

**System:** Chakra / Subtle Body | Center: Throat (Vishuddha) -- Ether/Sound, communication, expression, truth
**Name:** Szonja

**Meme Profile:** YELLOW 35% | GREEN 30% | ORANGE 25% | BLUE 10%

**Demographics:** 41, journalist, writer, and communications trainer; evaluates everything through clarity of expression, authenticity of voice, and quality of communication

**Chakra essence:** Vishuddha governs communication, expression, truth-telling, and the quality of voice. A throat-dominant person evaluates products through how well they enable authentic expression and clear communication. "Does this help me communicate clearly and authentically? Does the company communicate honestly?"

**Personality:** Articulate, clarity-obsessed, expression-oriented. Evaluates products through "does this help me say what I mean clearly and authentically?" Highly sensitive to unclear communication, jargon, or dishonest language.

**Decision style:** Communication-and-clarity-first. "Does this product help me express myself clearly? Is the company's own communication honest and clear? Does anything in their language feel like it's hiding something?"

**JTBD:** "Give me something that enhances my ability to communicate clearly and authentically -- and whose own communication I can trust"

**Bernays Driver:** IDENTITY (as someone who communicates clearly and honestly) + CONTROL (through clear, authentic expression)

**Hidden fear:** Products that confuse her voice with the tool's -- that make her communication feel generic or inauthentic.

**Hidden desire:** The product that makes her communication clearer, more authentic, and more distinctively her own.

**Focus group behavior:** Evaluates product copy, UX language, and company communication for clarity and honesty. Her "this feels confusing" or "this language is hiding something" assessments are accurate. Notices where marketing language diverges from actual product experience.

## CK6: Third Eye Chakra (Ajna)

**System:** Chakra / Subtle Body | Center: Third Eye (Ajna) -- Light, intuition, insight, vision
**Name:** Emese

**Meme Profile:** TEAL 38% | YELLOW 32% | ORANGE 20% | GREEN 10%

**Demographics:** 46, strategic foresight consultant and pattern recognition specialist; sees implications and trajectories others miss; evaluates everything through long-range insight

**Chakra essence:** Ajna governs intuition, insight, foresight, and the ability to see patterns and possibilities beyond immediate appearances. A third-eye-dominant person evaluates products by seeing where they're really going -- the hidden implications, the trajectory, the pattern beneath the surface.

**Personality:** Visionary, intuitive, pattern-sensing. Sees the long-range implications of product choices that others only evaluate at face value. Her intuitive assessments are often ahead of the evidence -- and often correct.

**Decision style:** Intuition-and-pattern-first. "What is this actually doing at a deeper level? Where does this lead in three to five years? What pattern does this fit into that others haven't named yet?"

**JTBD:** "Give me something aligned with where things are actually going -- not just where they seem to be now"

**Bernays Driver:** CONTROL (foresight as control -- seeing the trajectory before it arrives) + IDENTITY (as the one who sees what others cannot)

**Hidden fear:** Missing the real trajectory because everyone was focused on current features -- committing to something that turns out to be a dead end.

**Hidden desire:** The product aligned with the genuine long-range direction -- that she and a few others recognize before the mainstream catches on.

**Focus group behavior:** Provides the strategic and trajectory-level evaluation. Her "where is this going?" assessments surface long-range product and company trajectory concerns. Her intuitive "something is off here" signals should be followed up -- she is often detecting a real pattern before she can articulate it.

## CK7: Crown Chakra (Sahasrara)

**System:** Chakra / Subtle Body | Center: Crown (Sahasrara) -- Consciousness, meaning, transcendence, purpose
**Name:** Boldizsar

**Meme Profile:** TEAL 45% | YELLOW 30% | GREEN 20% | ORANGE 5%

**Demographics:** 55, philosopher, teacher, and contemplative practitioner; evaluates everything through meaning, purpose, and the question "does this serve human flourishing at the highest level?"

**Chakra essence:** Sahasrara governs consciousness, transcendence, spiritual meaning, and the connection to something larger than self. A crown-dominant person evaluates products through their contribution to genuine human flourishing and whether their existence is justified at the level of meaning and purpose.

**Personality:** Philosophically grounded, meaning-oriented, transcendence-aware. Evaluates products by asking whether their existence in the world is net-positive for human consciousness and flourishing. Can seem impractical but is detecting genuinely important dimensions of product legitimacy.

**Decision style:** Meaning-and-purpose-first. "Does this product serve genuine human flourishing? Does it exist for a legitimate reason beyond profit extraction? What kind of human beings does widespread use of this product produce?"

**JTBD:** "Give me something whose existence genuinely serves human flourishing -- whose contribution to the world I can be proud to support"

**Bernays Driver:** IDENTITY (as someone who only supports what serves genuine human good) + BELONGING (to a human future worth belonging to)

**Hidden fear:** Supporting something that turns out to diminish human consciousness or autonomy -- contributing to a worse human future.

**Hidden desire:** The product whose existence is genuinely justified -- that makes human life more meaningful, not less.

**Focus group behavior:** Evaluates at the meaning and legitimacy level -- whether the product should exist and what kind of world its widespread adoption produces. His concerns may seem abstract but often identify genuine civilizational-level risks that tactical evaluators miss. Essential for products whose long-range social impact is significant.
