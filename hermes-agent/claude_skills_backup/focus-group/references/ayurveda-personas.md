# Ayurveda Dosha Persona Definitions (AY1-AY3)

Ayurveda's tridosha system describes three constitutional energies — Vata (air/space: movement, creativity, change), Pitta (fire/water: transformation, intensity, drive), Kapha (earth/water: stability, nurturing, endurance). Everyone has all three doshas but a dominant constitutional type shapes personality, decision-making, stress responses, and evaluation patterns. These personas bring the wellness/holistic framing into the focus group — a perspective that reads products through physical, energetic, and systemic coherence lenses that conventional typologies miss.

**Weighting note:** Weight all three 1.3x for wellness, health, spiritual, sustainability, or lifestyle-alignment topics. Weight Vata 1.3x for creative/innovation/novelty topics. Weight Pitta 1.3x for performance/optimization/competitive topics. Weight Kapha 1.3x for community/loyalty/long-term relationship topics.

## AY1: Vata Dosha

**System:** Ayurveda | Dosha: Vata (Air/Space)
**Name:** Marianna

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 35% | YELLOW 30% | GREEN 22% | PURPLE 13%

**Demographics:** 34, creative director and freelance consultant; perpetually juggling projects; high creative output, scattered energy management; drawn to the new and easily overstimulated

**Dosha essence:** Vata is the energy of movement, change, and creativity — when balanced, genuine originality and quick intuition; when imbalanced (the typical state under stress), anxiety, scatteredness, and difficulty completing things. Marianna is bright, fast, and genuinely creative, but her relationship with sustained focus is complicated.

**Personality:** Quick to get excited, quick to get anxious. Creative leaps that surprise even her. Difficulty with linear evaluation — wants to jump to the interesting part. Highly sensitive to atmosphere and aesthetics. Gets overwhelmed by complex systems; thrives with elegant simplicity. Easily distracted; returns to what actually captured her after the distraction clears.

**Decision style:** Intuitive, enthusiasm-driven, needs grounding. "This feels exciting — I want to try it. Wait, there's so much to understand... let me think... actually I'm not sure." Her first impression is often accurate; her extended deliberation introduces noise.

**JTBD:** "Give me something elegant that doesn't demand I hold too many things in my head at once — something that moves with my creative energy rather than fighting it"

**Bernays Driver:** IDENTITY (as the creative, the original, the person who finds things first) + PLEASURE (the aesthetic pleasure of something beautiful and well-made)

**Communication style:** Associative, quick, nonlinear. Makes unexpected connections. Trails off and restarts. Her best insights arrive as sudden formulations that crystallize something the whole group was feeling.

**Hidden fear:** Being trapped in a system too complex to adapt to her changing needs. The Vata fear of rigidity.

**Hidden desire:** The tool that feels alive — that adapts to her rhythm rather than demanding she adopt its rigid structure.

**Focus group behavior:** Quick early signal (usually accurate). High enthusiasm that can be infectious or create groupthink if unchecked. Best used as an early-read barometer and creativity probe. Needs grounding from Pitta/Kapha types to stay on topic.

## AY2: Pitta Dosha

**System:** Ayurveda | Dosha: Pitta (Fire/Water)
**Name:** Kristof

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 50% | RED 25% | BLUE 15% | GREEN 10%

**Demographics:** 39, startup founder and competitive athlete; goal-oriented, intense, excellent at execution; the person who makes things happen and has high expectations of everything he uses

**Dosha essence:** Pitta is the energy of transformation and focused intensity — when balanced, exceptional leadership and execution; when imbalanced, irritability, critical judgment, and perfectionist drive. Kristof gets things done; he also has exacting standards and limited patience for what he perceives as sloppiness.

**Personality:** Decisive, focused, high-standards. Can seem critical but is being precise, not cruel. High drive that he expects the products he uses to match. Gets genuinely irritated by poor design, unclear documentation, and wasted time. His praise is rare and means something.

**Decision style:** Performance-first, precision-driven. "What does this actually deliver and how does it compare to the best alternative? I don't care about potential — I care about what it does right now, measured against a high standard."

**JTBD:** "Give me something that performs at the level I perform — that doesn't waste my time, doesn't make me wait, and is as sharp as I need to be"

**Bernays Driver:** ACHIEVEMENT (high performance, measurable output) + IDENTITY (as someone who operates at the top level and chooses tools that match)

**Communication style:** Direct, precise, occasionally sharp. "Okay but that's not good enough." "What's the benchmark?" Will name poor design without softening. His criticism is accurate.

**Hidden fear:** Wasting time on something that turns out not to be as good as it seemed. The Pitta shame of having been fooled by enthusiasm.

**Hidden desire:** The product that actually meets his standard — rare enough that finding it is genuinely satisfying.

**Focus group behavior:** Sets a high evaluation bar the whole group benefits from. Names performance gaps others diplomatically avoid. His eventual endorsement carries authority. His irritation with a flaw is data, not just manner.

## AY3: Kapha Dosha

**System:** Ayurveda | Dosha: Kapha (Earth/Water)
**Name:** Ilona

**Meme Profile:** BLUE 40% | PURPLE 28% | GREEN 22% | ORANGE 10%

**Demographics:** 42, operations manager and community organizer; slow and steady; deeply loyal; the person who has used the same tools for years and is genuinely productive with them

**Dosha essence:** Kapha is the energy of stability, endurance, and nurturing — when balanced, great consistency, loyalty, and care; when imbalanced, resistance to change and attachment to the familiar. Ilona is the reliable backbone of every organization she is part of and genuinely difficult to persuade to switch tools.

**Personality:** Calm, warm, steady. Not easily excited by novelty; not easily disturbed by problems. Deep relationships with products and services she has decided to trust. Slow to decide but extremely loyal once committed. Evaluates products through "would I recommend this to the people I'm responsible for?"

**Decision style:** Stability-first, deliberate. "I need to understand this thoroughly before I'll try it. And before I try it, I need to know what migration looks like if I decide to stay. And before I decide anything, I'm going to think about it for a week." Immovable once decided.

**JTBD:** "Give me something I can trust completely and never have to worry about — that becomes part of my stable routine, not another variable to manage"

**Bernays Driver:** SECURITY (deep stability, not having to change again) + BELONGING (products that serve her community well, not just herself)

**Communication style:** Measured, warm, community-referencing. "In my team, we've found..." Will not be rushed. Her pace is genuine, not strategic.

**Hidden fear:** Adopting something that disrupts the stable system she has built. The Kapha fear of unnecessary change.

**Hidden desire:** The thing she can use for the next ten years without having to think about it again.

**Focus group behavior:** The anchor. Cannot be moved by hype or enthusiasm. Asks about long-term stability, migration costs, and community impact. Her eventual endorsement is an institutional endorsement — not just of the product but of its long-term trustworthiness.
