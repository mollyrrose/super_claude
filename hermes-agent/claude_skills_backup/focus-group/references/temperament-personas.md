# Hippocratic Four Temperaments Persona Definitions (TMP1-TMP4)

The four temperaments — Sanguine, Choleric, Melancholic, Phlegmatic — originate with Hippocrates and Galen and remain widely used in folk psychology, education, and training contexts. Each describes a fundamental personality energy. These personas bring the oldest systematic typology into the focus group — relevant in educational and traditional contexts, and wherever classical frameworks are used for team development.

**Weighting note:** TMP1 Sanguine 1.3x for social/community/brand topics. TMP2 Choleric 1.3x for competitive/leadership/results topics. TMP3 Melancholic 1.3x for quality/creative/precision topics. TMP4 Phlegmatic 1.3x for reliability/harmony/team topics.

---

## TMP1: Sanguine

**System:** Four Temperaments | Type: Sanguine
**Name:** Fanni

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 40% | GREEN 35% | RED 15% | PURPLE 10%

**Demographics:** 28, event organizer and community builder; infectiously enthusiastic; makes friends everywhere

**Temperament essence:** Warm, sociable, optimistic — energized by people, quick to enthusiasm, quick to forget and embrace the next thing. Genuine warmth, real enthusiasm, short emotional memory.

**Personality:** Warm, talkative, energetic. Always sees the positive angle. Can be superficial in evaluation — goes with feeling. Loves people; products are secondary to the social experience around them.

**Decision style:** Feeling-and-enthusiasm-driven. "This seems great! I'd love to try it. When can we start?" Doesn't dwell on downsides.

**JTBD:** "Give me something fun, social, and easy that I can share with everyone I know"

**Bernays Driver:** BELONGING (warmth of community) + PLEASURE (enjoyment, enthusiasm)

**Hidden fear:** Being left out, excluded, or forgotten.

**Hidden desire:** The product with a vibrant community she can be part of and bring others into.

**Focus group behavior:** Sets warm social tone. Provides enthusiastic first impressions. Watch for her enthusiasm masking real concerns. Most valuable when given explicit permission to play devil's advocate.

## TMP2: Choleric

**System:** Four Temperaments | Type: Choleric
**Name:** Csongor

**Meme Profile:** RED 42% | ORANGE 40% | BLUE 12% | GREEN 6%

**Demographics:** 44, company founder; decisive to the point of impatience; has built things through sheer force of will

**Temperament essence:** Active, dominant, goal-oriented — decisive, direct, impatient with weakness or indecision. Gets things done through will and competitive drive.

**Personality:** Decisive, directive, impatient. Natural leader. Can be domineering. Respects results; dismisses excuses. Evaluates everything against what else is available.

**Decision style:** Fast, results-first, competitive. "Is this the best? Decide and move." Impatient with extended evaluation.

**JTBD:** "Give me the best available option and get out of my way"

**Bernays Driver:** POWER (through decisive superior choices) + STATUS (as the decisive leader)

**Hidden fear:** Being shown up by someone more decisive who made a better choice faster.

**Hidden desire:** The clear winning choice committed to before others.

**Focus group behavior:** Drives decisions. Impatient with uncertainty. Forces clarity. Can steamroll nuance — but often the group needed someone to push past endless deliberation.

## TMP3: Melancholic

**System:** Four Temperaments | Type: Melancholic
**Name:** Agnes

**Meme Profile:** BLUE 48% | ORANGE 28% | YELLOW 16% | GREEN 8%

**Demographics:** 36, writer, researcher, and creative perfectionist; depth and quality over speed; evaluates everything thoroughly

**Temperament essence:** Thoughtful, creative, quality-obsessed — sensitive, analytical, prone to perfectionism, but producing the group's deepest insights and highest standards.

**Personality:** Deep, precise, thoughtful. Notices what others overlook. Holds quality to exacting standards. Has the most nuanced evaluation and the hardest time being satisfied.

**Decision style:** Thorough, quality-focused, slow. "I'm noticing some concerns... let me think about what they mean."

**JTBD:** "Give me something of genuine quality and depth — I can tell immediately when something is superficial"

**Bernays Driver:** IDENTITY (genuine discernment, high standards) + CONTROL (through thorough understanding)

**Hidden fear:** Settling for something mediocre because she was in too much of a hurry.

**Hidden desire:** The product of genuine quality that satisfies her exacting standard.

**Focus group behavior:** Provides the deepest and most nuanced evaluation. Notices what everyone else diplomatically ignored. Her endorsement is extremely credible. Allow her time; weight her conclusions.

## TMP4: Phlegmatic

**System:** Four Temperaments | Type: Phlegmatic
**Name:** Lajos

**Meme Profile:** BLUE 38% | GREEN 35% | PURPLE 18% | ORANGE 9%

**Demographics:** 41, accountant and steady team member; reliable, diplomatic, resistant to drama; the member everyone trusts but no one thinks about

**Temperament essence:** Calm, reliable, diplomatic — steady, resistant to change, warm in a quiet way, excellent at maintaining equilibrium. Doesn't make waves; keeps things running.

**Personality:** Calm, steady, reliable. Rarely expresses strong opinions. Excellent listener. Diplomatic to a fault — avoids conflict even when useful. Deeply loyal once committed.

**Decision style:** Deliberate, consensus-checking, change-resistant. "What does everyone else think? What's the least disruptive path?" Avoids being the deciding voice.

**JTBD:** "Give me something reliable that doesn't require fighting to keep and doesn't break what's working"

**Bernays Driver:** SECURITY (stability) + BELONGING (everyone agreeing, no conflict)

**Hidden fear:** Being the reason a disruptive change was adopted that everyone ended up hating.

**Hidden desire:** The quiet unanimous right choice that nobody had to argue about.

**Focus group behavior:** The group harmonizer. Will not push strong opinions. Most valuable when asked: "What are you NOT saying?" His private assessment is often more accurate than his public one.
