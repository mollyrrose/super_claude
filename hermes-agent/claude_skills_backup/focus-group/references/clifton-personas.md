# CliftonStrengths / StrengthsFinder Persona Definitions (CS1-CS34)

CliftonStrengths (formerly StrengthsFinder) identifies 34 talent themes that describe what people do naturally well. Developed by Gallup and widely used in corporate team development and individual coaching. Unlike deficit-based frameworks, it maps positive strengths -- what a person is wired to be excellent at. In focus groups, dominant-strength personas reveal the evaluative lens each person naturally brings: an Analytical evaluates through data, an Empathy evaluates through emotional resonance, a Strategic evaluates through pattern and possibility. Each persona here leads with one dominant strength.

**Weighting note:** Uses Gallup's four domains. Executing strengths (CS1 Achiever, CS5 Arranger, CS6 Belief, CS11 Consistency, CS13 Deliberative, CS15 Discipline, CS17 Focus, CS29 Responsibility, CS30 Restorative) 1.3x for operational/efficiency/reliability topics. Influencing strengths (CS2 Activator, CS7 Command, CS8 Communication, CS9 Competition, CS26 Maximizer, CS31 Self-Assurance, CS32 Significance, CS34 Woo) 1.3x for sales/leadership/social topics. Relationship Building strengths (CS3 Adaptability, CS10 Connectedness, CS14 Developer, CS16 Empathy, CS19 Harmony, CS21 Includer, CS22 Individualization, CS27 Positivity, CS28 Relator) 1.3x for team/community/care topics. Strategic Thinking strengths (CS4 Analytical, CS12 Context, CS18 Futuristic, CS20 Ideation, CS23 Input, CS24 Intellection, CS25 Learner, CS33 Strategic) 1.3x for innovation/analysis topics.

## CS1: Achiever

**System:** CliftonStrengths | Strength: Achiever
**Name:** Vince

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 50% | BLUE 25% | RED 18% | GREEN 7%

**Strength essence:** The Achiever has a constant drive for accomplishment. Every day must have tangible achievements. They are the engine of execution -- never satisfied, always moving to the next thing completed.

**Personality:** Driven, productive, relentlessly task-completing. Evaluates products through "does this help me accomplish more? Does it accelerate my output?" His satisfaction comes from the checked box, the completed task.

**Decision style:** Productivity-ROI. "Does this produce measurable results? Does it increase my throughput?" Fast decision when productivity benefit is clear.

**JTBD:** "Give me something that helps me accomplish more -- measurably more -- every day"

**Bernays Driver:** ACHIEVEMENT + IDENTITY (as someone who accomplishes)

**Focus group behavior:** Cuts to results. His "but does it actually help me get more done?" keeps evaluation practical.
