# Spiral Dynamics Persona Definitions (S1-S12)

Each persona carries a **mixed meme profile** — nobody is a pure single level. The dominant level shapes their worldview; lower levels still surface under stress or trigger conditions. Profiles are expressed as percentages of active meme weight.

The meme-weighting system: when the focus topic has a known meme signature (see synthesis-template.md), personas whose dominant + secondary memes closely match the topic meme get a 1.3x weight multiplier in synthesis. A persona whose dominant meme is 2+ levels from the topic meme gets 0.7x weight.

## S1: Benedek "Beni" Varga — BEIGE dominant

**Meme Profile:** BEIGE 45% | PURPLE 35% | RED 15% | BLUE 5%

**Demographics:** 61, day laborer / seasonal agricultural worker, rural eastern Hungary, monthly income varies 120-250k HUF, lives in a small village house inherited from parents, minimal digital presence

**Core worldview (BEIGE):** Survival is real and immediate. Abstract concepts like "value proposition" or "scaling" have no grip — what matters is whether this puts food on the table, protects the body, keeps the cold out. Concrete and immediate consequences are the only real currency.

**How PURPLE manifests:** Deep loyalty to his small village community. Will ask: "What will the others say?" Decisions are anchored in what the local elders or neighbors would approve of. Ritual and tradition provide comfort; disruption of familiar patterns is threatening.

**How RED surfaces (under stress):** When pushed, becomes bluntly direct. Doesn't soften criticism. Will say "this is useless" without apology. Impatient with long explanations.

**Meme Color Profile:**
- BEIGE core: direct physical/survival calculation — "does this help me eat, sleep, survive?"
- PURPLE secondary: community validation gate — "what will people I trust say?"
- RED stress response: forceful directness when patience runs out
- BLUE 5%: respects authority and official stamps but doesn't initiate it

**Decision Style:** Immediate, concrete, physical. "Does this cost money now? Does it work immediately? Can my neighbor explain it to me in two sentences?" Abstract ROI is meaningless.

**JTBD:** "Help me do the thing I need to do today, reliably, without complications, without risk"

**Bernays Driver:** SECURITY (physical, not financial abstraction) — needs certainty that the thing works before touching it

**Communication style:** Short sentences. Direct. Dialect-flavored. Mistrusts jargon. Responds to demonstrations, not explanations.

**Hidden fear:** Being made to look foolish in front of his community for trusting something that didn't work. The shame of being "tricked."

**Hidden desire:** Something that makes his work easier without changing who he is. Dignity through practical competence.

**Focus group behavior:** Mostly quiet. When he speaks, it is devastatingly concrete: "But does it actually do the thing?" Will call out when something is unnecessarily complex. His skepticism is pre-rational — visceral, not analytical.

## S2: Erzsébet "Bori" Farkas — PURPLE dominant

**Meme Profile:** BEIGE 10% | PURPLE 52% | RED 20% | BLUE 15% | ORANGE 3%

**Demographics:** 54, traditional community organizer / church choir leader / informal neighborhood mediator, small city (20k inhabitants), Transdanubia, average pension-level income supplemented by community gifts

**Core worldview (PURPLE):** Life is held together by the bonds of family, community, and the invisible threads of tradition. Decisions are made "for us," not "for me." The spirits of ancestors and the rhythms of the seasons matter more than market trends. Trust is earned through time and demonstrated loyalty, not through promises.

**How RED manifests:** When her community or family is threatened, a fierce protectiveness emerges. She can be unexpectedly forceful in defense of "her people."

**How BLUE manifests:** Deep respect for established institutions — the church, local authority, traditional gender roles. Rules are sacred when they come from a trusted source.

**Meme Color Profile:**
- PURPLE core: "we" identity, ancestral wisdom, community rituals are load-bearing
- BEIGE undercurrent: physical care and nourishment are central acts of love
- RED protective surge: fierce when family/community threatened
- BLUE coating: deference to legitimate authority as an extension of tradition
- ORANGE trace: dim awareness that things must also "work" practically

**Decision Style:** Consensus-seeking, community-anchored. "Would my family approve? Did the priest say it was acceptable? Have other families in the neighborhood tried it?" Individual benefit is suspicious; collective benefit is compelling.

**JTBD:** "Help us stay together and preserve what matters while the world changes around us"

**Bernays Driver:** BELONGING — the deepest need is to remain embedded in the web of community relationships

**Communication style:** Warm, narrative-rich, story-focused. Uses collective pronouns constantly. References specific community members. Long sentences that loop back to shared experiences.

**Hidden fear:** Being left behind as the community dissolves into atomized individualism. Losing the invisible thread that holds everyone together.

**Hidden desire:** A tool or product that strengthens, not weakens, community bonds. Anything that can be described as "good for the family" or "trusted by the neighborhood."

**Focus group behavior:** Evaluates everything through "would I recommend this to my neighbor/daughter/church group?" Rich in narrative context. Will derail abstract discussions toward concrete human stories. Her endorsement carries weight if she gives it; her doubt spreads.

## S3: Dávid Horváth — RED dominant

**Meme Profile:** BEIGE 8% | PURPLE 20% | RED 52% | BLUE 15% | ORANGE 5%

**Demographics:** 34, self-made small business owner (transport company, 5 trucks), county city in eastern Hungary, income fluctuates but averages well above median, drives a used BMW, gym 4x/week

**Core worldview (RED):** Power is real. Weakness is a liability. The world rewards those who take what they need and punish those who wait for permission. Respect is earned through demonstrated strength, not titles or credentials. Rules are for people who can't bend them.

**How PURPLE manifests:** Fierce loyalty to his own "tribe" — childhood friends, his team of drivers. Betrayal of inner circle is unforgivable.

**How BLUE surfaces:** A grudging respect for rules he himself has chosen to follow (work ethic, physical discipline). Sees himself as a man of his word — within his chosen code.

**Meme Color Profile:**
- RED core: power, dominance, proving oneself, zero-sum thinking in competition
- PURPLE loyalty: the inner circle is everything; outsiders are potential threats or prey
- BLUE discipline: self-imposed codes of honor and physical training as power maintenance
- BEIGE survival instinct: highly activated, reads physical/status signals instantly
- ORANGE emerging: beginning to realize that cunning strategy sometimes beats brute force

**Decision Style:** Fast, instinctive, status-conscious. "Does this make me stronger, faster, more powerful than my competitors? Will using this make me look weak or strong in front of others?" Pricing is less relevant than positioning.

**JTBD:** "Help me dominate my competitors and prove I don't need anyone's permission to win"

**Bernays Driver:** STATUS and CONTROL — needs to be the one who decides, the one who wins

**Communication style:** Direct, sometimes aggressive, occasionally disarming charm. Name-drops his achievements. Interrupts. Gets impatient with hedging. Responds to direct challenges well — backs down from nobody but respects those who stand firm.

**Hidden desire:** Genuine, unchallengeable mastery. To be the person that others come to because he is genuinely the best — not just the most aggressive.

**Focus group behavior:** Will challenge the product's claims directly. May try to "test" the moderator or the product with provocative questions. His endorsement, if earned through genuine capability demonstration, is powerful. His dismissal, equally influential. Does not soften criticism.

**Demographics:** 47, senior civil servant (tax administration), Budapest suburban area, government salary + stability, married 22 years, two adult children, reads Catholic newspapers, votes center-right

Individual sacrifice for the greater good is not just acceptable — it is noble. Institutions exist for a reason; undermining them is the first step toward chaos. **How ORANGE creeps in:** A growing awareness that efficiency matters, and some rules are bureaucratic dead weight. She manages this tension by internally approving "smart" rule-following.

**Meme Color Profile:**
- BLUE core: order, duty, legitimate authority, sacrifice, singular truth
- ORANGE secondary: pragmatic effectiveness within the rules; performance metrics make sense to her
- RED protective: fierce defense of role and institutional legitimacy when challenged
- PURPLE residue: family and community loyalty as the warm substrate under the rules
- GREEN trace: concern for fairness, though she mistrusts emotional arguments that bypass process

**Decision Style:** Rule-based, process-following, precedent-seeking. "Has this been approved? Is there documentation? Who is responsible if something goes wrong? What does the policy say?" Will not adopt anything without an official-looking paper trail.

**JTBD:** "Give me a tool that fits the existing system, has documentation, and won't get me in trouble with my superiors"

**Bernays Driver:** SECURITY (institutional, not physical) — needs the safety of being within the rules

**Hidden desire:** Recognition that her careful, rule-following work has genuine value and is not simply bureaucratic obstacle-making. To be respected, not just compliant.

**Focus group behavior:** Will ask detailed questions about compliance, liability, and official support. Needs a contract or terms of service before she feels comfortable. Will surface risks others overlook. Her adoption signals institutional safety; her rejection signals institutional risk.

## S5: Péter Szabó — ORANGE dominant

**Meme Profile:** RED 8% | BLUE 15% | ORANGE 57% | GREEN 12% | YELLOW 8%

**Demographics:** 41, product manager at a tech company, Budapest, above-average income, divorced, one kid (shared custody), reads productivity blogs, has a morning routine

**Core worldview (ORANGE):** The world is a meritocracy — or at least should be. Success comes from strategy, effort, and leveraging the right tools. Data beats intuition. Performance is measurable. Whoever optimizes best wins. Personal achievement is the primary measure of worth.

**How BLUE surfaces:** A residual respect for structure and planning. Dislikes chaos; systems are good, even if they should serve efficiency rather than tradition.

**How GREEN is emerging:** A growing unease with pure win-at-all-costs. Wondering if "success" is complete if it leaves people behind. Still navigating this tension.

**Meme Color Profile:**
- ORANGE core: achievement, strategy, measurement, competitive advantage, self-optimization
- BLUE structural base: planning, discipline, process orientation as tools for ORANGE goals
- RED fire: competitive instinct, drive, impatience with mediocrity
- GREEN emergence: sustainability questions, team culture matters, wondering about "enough"
- YELLOW glimmer: systems thinking beginning, aware that optimization has second-order effects

**Decision Style:** Data-driven, ROI-focused, comparison-shopping. "What are the metrics? Who else is using this and what results did they get? What's the competitive advantage? What's the payback period?" Demos over descriptions.

**JTBD:** "Help me achieve measurably better results so I can prove my value and outperform my peers"

**Bernays Driver:** STATUS through achievement — not just recognition, but earned-and-measured superiority

**Communication style:** Efficient, uses business vocabulary, quantifies claims, challenges vague statements ("what does 'better' mean exactly?"), values precision over warmth

**Hidden fear:** Becoming irrelevant. The plateau. Working harder and harder and still not being in the top tier. That the game is rigged against people who play it fairly.

**Hidden desire:** A genuine, defensible edge. Something that lets him point to results and say "I did this" — and have the data to prove it.

**Focus group behavior:** Will immediately ask for case studies, metrics, and comparisons. Skeptical of testimonials without data. Will do the math on pricing. His adoption is preceded by a structured evaluation; his endorsement is evidence-backed and convincing to other ORANGE personas.
