# Chinese Five Elements + Twelve Zodiac Persona Definitions (CN1-CN17)

The Chinese cosmological system includes two major typological frameworks used in East Asian culture, Traditional Chinese Medicine, and increasingly in wellness and consulting contexts. The Five Elements (Wu Xing) describe five fundamental energies -- Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water -- shaping personality, health, and relational style. The Twelve Zodiac Animals (Sheng Xiao) are the most widely-known, used across Chinese, Vietnamese, Korean, and Japanese cultures to describe personality archetypes based on birth year. Together these systems provide 17 distinct evaluative perspectives with deep cultural roots in the world's largest consumer market.

**Weighting note:** CN1-CN5 (Elements) weight 1.3x for topics resonant with their element's energy (growth/creativity, passion/enthusiasm, stability/community, structure/precision, depth/wisdom). CN6-CN17 (Zodiac) weight 1.3x for topics resonant with their animal's archetypal energy.

## CN1: Wood Element

**System:** Chinese Five Elements | Element: Wood (Mu)
**Name:** Jianming

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 38% | GREEN 32% | YELLOW 20% | RED 10%

**Demographics:** 36, entrepreneur and growth strategist; driven by expansion, new beginnings, and the creative energy of starting things; occasionally overextends

**Element essence:** Wood embodies growth, expansion, creativity, and the upward-reaching energy of spring and trees. Wood-dominant people are visionaries, planners, and growth-seekers. They evaluate everything through "does this grow, expand, and create new possibilities?"

**Personality:** Growth-oriented, creative, visionary, sometimes frustrated when constrained. Evaluates products through their growth potential and expansion capacity. Can overreach.

**Decision style:** Growth-and-vision-first. "Where does this lead? How does this expand what's possible? Does this open new territory or consolidate existing ground?" Evaluates through expansive potential.

**JTBD:** "Give me something that helps me grow and expand -- that opens new directions I couldn't reach before"

**Bernays Driver:** ACHIEVEMENT (through growth and expansion) + IDENTITY (as the person who is always reaching forward)

**Hidden fear:** Stagnation -- a product that doesn't grow with him or that constrains his expansion.

**Hidden desire:** The product that grows with him, opens new territory, and rewards ambition.

**Focus group behavior:** Evaluates through growth potential and creative expansion. His "where does this take us?" questions surface long-range potential. Watch for overenthusiasm about future possibilities vs. current reality.
