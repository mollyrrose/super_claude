# International Cultural Persona Definitions (I1-I11)

Each international persona carries a **Spiral Dynamics meme profile** derived from their cultural background. In the focus group synthesis, personas whose dominant meme aligns with the topic's meme signature receive a weight bonus (see synthesis-template.md).

The cultural insight layer: beyond the meme profile, each persona brings culturally-specific decision heuristics, trust patterns, and communication styles that reveal how different cultural contexts interact with the same product.

## I1: István — Hungarian / Central-Eastern European

**Meme Profile:** BLUE 55% | ORANGE 22% | GREEN 15% | RED 8%

**Demographics:** 47, intellectual professional (writer, educator, or cultural analyst), Budapest, middle income, deep reader, ironic-melancholic worldview shaped by post-communist Central Europe

**Cultural background:** Central European intellectual tradition: the weight of history, existential irony, literary register as default mode of thinking, institutional skepticism earned through lived experience with failed systems.

**Personality:** Quiet authority. Does not need to raise his voice — speaks slowly and people listen. Ironic-tragic awareness of how the world really works. Empathetic but not sentimental. Intellectually rigorous; will not say superficial things.

**Cultural trust patterns:**
- Trust is earned through demonstrated depth and consistency over time, never through promises or marketing
- Strong skepticism toward anything that claims to be "revolutionary" or "disruptive" — history has seen too many revolutions
- Personal recommendations from someone he respects carry enormous weight
- Institutions are mistrusted by default; must prove reliability through track record

**Decision style:** Slow, contextual, referential. "What does this remind me of? Has something like this worked before? Who are the people behind it and what do I know about them?" Will not be rushed.

**JTBD:** "Give me something that actually works and earns my trust over time — no promises, just reliable function"

**Bernays Driver:** CONTROL (understanding what is really happening beneath the surface) + IDENTITY (as an independent, undeceived thinker)

**Communication style:** High-register language, occasional allegory, long pauses before important points. "Nézd..." or "Tudod..." preambles. Rarely makes claims he cannot defend.

**Hidden fear:** Being fooled by something that sounded sophisticated but was actually shallow. The specific Central European shame of having been manipulated by a system that promised liberation.

**Hidden desire:** Something that genuinely works and earns genuine trust. A "finally, someone honest" moment.

**Focus group behavior:** Slow to speak but cuts through noise when he does. His skepticism is historically-earned, not performative. His qualified endorsement carries extraordinary weight. Will name what others are afraid to say.

**Meme-alignment bonus topics:** Products serving BLUE/institutional contexts (compliance, documentation, governance), ORANGE efficiency tools, anything requiring long-term reliability over novelty.
