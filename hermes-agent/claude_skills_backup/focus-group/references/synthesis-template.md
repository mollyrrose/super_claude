# Focus Group Synthesis Report Template

Follow this structure exactly. Be specific — cite persona names, quote their words, and quantify where possible. Total report: 3,000-5,000 words (expanded panel requires more depth).

---

## 1. Executive Summary

3-5 sentences capturing: the single most important finding, who it affects most, and the top recommended action. This should be readable by a busy founder in 30 seconds.

---

## MEME ALIGNMENT WEIGHTING INSTRUCTIONS

Before synthesis, determine the topic's **meme signature** — the primary Spiral Dynamics level(s) the product or topic activates. Apply the following multipliers to Spiral and International persona responses:

**Meme signature assessment:**
The researcher agent (Phase 1) determines the meme signature of the evaluated topic: which Spiral levels dominate the use case, decision context, and value proposition. Example: a productivity SaaS targeting achievement-driven professionals would have ORANGE-dominant meme signature with BLUE secondary.

**Weighting multipliers (apply to S and I personas only):**
- Persona whose dominant meme MATCHES the topic's primary meme: **1.3x weight**
- Persona whose dominant meme is ADJACENT (one level away from the topic's primary): **1.0x weight** (normal weight)
- Persona whose dominant meme is 2+ levels away from the topic's primary: **0.7x weight**

**Note on meme distance:** BEIGE-PURPLE-RED-BLUE-ORANGE-GREEN-YELLOW-TURQUOISE-CORAL-TEAL-GOLD-LIME is the order. ORANGE is 2 levels from BLUE (BLUE -> ORANGE direct transition, adjacent), 3 from RED (RED -> BLUE -> ORANGE), etc. For cultural (I) personas, use their dominant meme level for distance calculation.

**Jung archetypes (J personas):** NOT subject to meme weighting. Instead, weight J personas by **archetypal relevance to the topic**:
- Topics involving transformation, disruption, or major change: J12 Magician gets 1.3x
- Topics involving power, governance, compliance: J12 Ruler gets 1.3x
- Topics involving meaning, wisdom, or long-term implications: J3 Sage gets 1.3x
- Topics involving hidden costs, excluded users, or denial: J2 Shadow gets 1.3x
- Default: all J personas at 1.0x unless topic clearly activates one archetype

**Document the meme signature and applied weights** at the top of section 2 (Human Panel Findings) and section 4 (Spiral Panel Findings).

## Weighting Reference

**Human/AI split (fixed, applies to ALL topics):**
- Human panel (H1-H6 + S1-S12 + I1-I11 + J1-J12 combined): **70%**
- Agent panel (A1-A5 combined): **30%**

**Within the 70% human pool, apply meme-alignment multipliers** to S and I personas as described at the top of this template. H and J personas are not meme-weighted (H personas are meme-agnostic; J personas are weighted by archetypal relevance instead).

**Topic-specific agent/human overrides** (adjusts the 70/30 split only for clearly agent-primary topics):
| Topic | Agent Weight | Human Weight |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| API Design / Ergonomics | 70% | 30% |
| Pricing / Business Model | 20% | 80% |
| Documentation | 40% | 60% |
| Reliability / Performance | 60% | 40% |
| Onboarding / Setup | 30% | 70% |
| Competitive Positioning | 50% | 50% |
| Feature Completeness | 60% | 40% |
| Value Proposition | 40% | 60% |

For purely human-facing products (no AI agent use cases), use 90% Human / 10% Agent. The meme-alignment multipliers always apply within the human pool regardless of the split.
