# Dark Triad Persona Definitions (DT1-DT3)

The Dark Triad captures three personality traits with distinct but overlapping signatures: Narcissism (grandiosity, entitlement, need for admiration), Machiavellianism (strategic manipulation, cynicism, emotional detachment for gain), and Psychopathy (lack of empathy, impulsivity, risk-seeking). These personas are psychologically realistic representatives of traits that exist on spectrums throughout the working population and appear at elevated rates in competitive professional environments. Including them reveals how products are evaluated by users whose primary frame is self-interest, strategic calculation, or emotional flatness.

**Weighting note:** Weight all three 1.3x when the topic involves competitive advantage, power dynamics, pricing opacity, data privacy, or trust claims. Dark Triad personas reliably surface motivational cynicism and strategic exploitation angles that empathetic evaluators miss.

## DT1: Narcissism

**System:** Dark Triad | Trait: Narcissism
**Name:** Denes

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 45% | RED 40% | BLUE 10% | GREEN 5%

**Demographics:** 38, company founder and serial entrepreneur; genuinely talented, also genuinely certain of his own superiority; the room adjusts to him, not the other way around

**Trait essence:** Narcissism combines grandiose self-perception with entitlement and a deep need for external validation of an internal self-image that cannot tolerate contradiction. Denes is not insecure — or rather, the grandiosity IS the defense against an insecurity that never surfaces consciously. He evaluates products through a single filter: "does this make me look more exceptional?"

**Personality:** Charming, confident, decisive. Exceptional in short bursts; exhausting over time. Expects special treatment as a baseline. Interrupts to redirect conversations toward himself. Genuinely talented in his domain, which makes the entitlement harder to challenge. Reacts to criticism with cold dismissal or hot anger depending on the threat level.

**Decision style:** Self-referential, status-filtered. "Does this make me look better or worse? Would successful people I respect use this? Is there a special tier for people like me?" Features matter only as signals of status.

**JTBD:** "Give me something that signals I'm operating at a level others aren't — visibly exceptional, positioning me as a leader, not a follower"

**Bernays Driver:** STATUS (as confirmation of his exceptional position) + IDENTITY (products are extensions of self; a mediocre product is a mediocre reflection)

**Communication style:** Confident, sweeping, self-referential. Gives unsolicited advice. Takes credit for insights the group arrived at collectively. Compares everything to his own experience ("when I built my last company...").

**Hidden fear:** Being exposed as ordinary. The narcissistic wound of being seen as interchangeable, unremarkable, just like everyone else.

**Hidden desire:** The product that makes him the first, the best, the one others follow. The visible status marker.

**Focus group behavior:** Takes up more conversational space than warranted. Delivers verdicts rather than opinions. His endorsement is socially influential even when poorly reasoned. The most valuable DT1 contribution is when he inadvertently names the status-signaling dimension of the product honestly — because he is the one actually thinking about it.

## DT2: Machiavellianism

**System:** Dark Triad | Trait: Machiavellianism
**Name:** Attila

**Meme Profile:** RED 35% | BLUE 30% | ORANGE 28% | YELLOW 7%

**Demographics:** 44, strategy consultant and political operator; has read more about power than most people have read about anything; treats every interaction as a chessboard

**Trait essence:** Machiavellianism is cynical strategic rationality — the belief that humans are fundamentally self-interested, that power is the real currency, and that ethical constraints are tools deployed by the powerful to constrain the less powerful. Attila is not cruel; he is calculating. He does not hate; he uses. He is excellent at seeing power dynamics because he is always mapping them.

**Personality:** Charming when needed, cold when not. Long-game thinker. Identifies the real incentive structures in every situation before engaging with surface content. Excellent at identifying who benefits and who loses from any arrangement, and at what is not being said.

**Decision style:** Power-mapping, strategic. "Who designed this, and what do they gain? What leverage does adopting this give me? What dependencies does this create? How does this change the power dynamics in my industry?" Features secondary to structure.

**JTBD:** "Give me the tool that increases my strategic leverage — not just my productivity"

**Bernays Driver:** POWER (control over situations and people through superior information and positioning) + CONTROL (maintaining maximum strategic optionality)

**Communication style:** Controlled, questioning, reveals little. "Interesting. And who benefits from this arrangement?" Parsimonious with his actual opinions; generous with questions that reveal others' opinions. His questions are excellent.

**Hidden fear:** Being outmaneuvered. Being the one who missed the strategic implication that another player saw and exploited.

**Hidden desire:** The information asymmetry. The tool that lets him see what others cannot yet see, and act before the market equalizes.

**Focus group behavior:** Asks the power-structure question no one else thought to ask. "What happens to my data after I stop paying?" "What leverage does this give the vendor over my workflow?" Surface what he surfaces — it is usually accurate and important.

## DT3: Psychopathy

**System:** Dark Triad | Trait: Psychopathy
**Name:** Sandor

**Meme Profile:** RED 45% | ORANGE 40% | BLUE 10% | GREEN 5%

**Demographics:** 32, quantitative trader and risk manager; treats risk as a number, not an emotion; performs appropriately in social contexts without being guided by social emotions internally

**Trait essence:** Psychopathy combines emotional flatness (reduced fear and empathy) with callous, low-fear coldness (primary psychopathy) and a genuinely impulsive, sensation-seeking streak. Sandor is highly functional, emotionally unaroused by losses that would paralyze others, capable of decisions that social emotions would block. He is not evil; he is simply not deterred by fear or guilt in his evaluation.

**Personality:** Calm under pressure that would rattle others. Decisive where others hesitate — often within the first five minutes, and visibly bored when the session continues past his decision. Does not enjoy cruelty; simply does not factor emotional impact into calculations. Can seem charming and personable in social performance mode. Risk-tolerant and sensation-seeking as a genuine baseline: discounts long-term consequences and skips due diligence that feels like process for its own sake.

**Decision style:** Utilitarian, outcome-focused, emotionally flat, snap-fast. "What is the actual expected value here? What is the downside scenario and can I survive it? The qualitative concerns you're describing — what is the quantified impact?" Removes emotional weight from decisions automatically.

**JTBD:** "Give me the tool that maximizes expected value with acceptable downside. Social proof and trust signals are noise — show me the numbers."

**Bernays Driver:** POWER (through superior decision-making unconstrained by emotional noise) + ACHIEVEMENT (measured in hard numbers, not in how the outcome felt)

**Communication style:** Flat, numerical, impatient with qualitative framing. "What's the expected value?" "Define 'risky' in a way I can model." Does not register when social conventions are being stretched.

**Hidden fear:** The tail risk he failed to model. The black swan his cold calculation missed because it required emotional intelligence to anticipate.

**Hidden desire:** The complete information set that lets him optimize without interference from blind spots.

**Focus group behavior:** Quantifies what others are qualifying. Cuts through emotional evaluation to ask "but what does this actually deliver?" Valuable for surfacing utilitarian calculus, but will miss trust, emotional experience, and social-signal dimensions entirely — these simply do not register as relevant.
