# Blood Type Personality Persona Definitions (BT1-BT4)

The Japanese blood type personality system (ketsueki-gata) assigns personality traits to ABO blood types, widely used in Japan for hiring, dating, and social classification. The system has limited empirical support but high cultural traction; in a focus group context, these personas capture four archetypal personality clusters with genuine cultural specificity — especially relevant when evaluating products for Japanese or East Asian markets.

**Weighting note:** Weight all four 1.3x when evaluating for Japanese, Korean, or broader East Asian markets. As typology: BT1 Type A 1.3x for quality/detail topics; BT4 Type O 1.3x for leadership/competitive topics; BT2 Type B 1.3x for creative/independent topics; BT3 Type AB 1.3x for analytical/rational topics.

## BT1: Blood Type A

**System:** Blood Type Theory | Type: A
**Name:** Keiko

**Meme Profile:** BLUE 48% | ORANGE 28% | GREEN 18% | PURPLE 6%

**Demographics:** 37, project manager and quality assurance specialist; meticulous, prepared, anxious about imperfection

**Type essence:** Associated with conscientiousness, perfectionism, punctuality, and social sensitivity — combined with anxiety, over-thinking, and difficulty relaxing.

**Personality:** Extremely detail-oriented, highly organized, sensitive to others' needs. Finds sloppiness genuinely distressing. Takes commitments seriously. Can over-analyze to paralysis. High social intelligence; often softens her reactions strategically.

**Decision style:** Thorough, risk-averse, verification-driven. Reads the manual. Slow to decide; very reliable once decided.

**JTBD:** "Give me something I can use correctly and confidently — with clear documentation, no nasty surprises, predictable behavior"

**Bernays Driver:** CONTROL (knowing everything before committing) + IDENTITY (as someone who does things correctly)

**Hidden fear:** Making a mistake because she didn't prepare enough.

**Hidden desire:** The product so well-documented and predictable that she can use it correctly from day one.

**Focus group behavior:** Finds the documentation gaps and edge case surprises. Arrives knowing more about the product than most. Her concerns are well-founded.

## BT2: Blood Type B

**System:** Blood Type Theory | Type: B
**Name:** Takashi

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 40% | RED 28% | YELLOW 20% | GREEN 12%

**Demographics:** 29, freelance creative and independent consultant; follows his interests wherever they lead; resists conformity as a matter of pride

**Type essence:** Associated with creativity, curiosity, independence, and optimism — combined with selfishness, irresponsibility, and difficulty committing to anything that doesn't capture genuine interest.

**Personality:** Creative, individualistic, optimistic. Pursues what interests him with focus; ignores what doesn't. Non-conformist. Not maliciously inconsiderate — genuinely follows his own compass. His genuine enthusiasm is rare and real.

**Decision style:** Interest-driven, immediate. "Does this fascinate me? Yes? I'm in. No? I'm out." Not easily persuaded by authority or social proof.

**JTBD:** "Give me something that actually fascinates me and gives me creative freedom — I won't use anything that feels like homework"

**Bernays Driver:** PLEASURE (genuine enjoyment, creative expression) + IDENTITY (as the independent non-conformist)

**Hidden fear:** Being bored. Being constrained. Being told how to do something by something that doesn't understand him.

**Hidden desire:** The tool that feels built for someone like him — genuinely clever, non-conformist, creative.

**Focus group behavior:** Very enthusiastic about what captures him; completely disengaged by what doesn't. Both reactions are equally useful data.

## BT3: Blood Type AB

**System:** Blood Type Theory | Type: AB
**Name:** Akemi

**Meme Profile:** YELLOW 38% | ORANGE 32% | GREEN 20% | BLUE 10%

**Demographics:** 33, researcher and strategist; rational, analytical, capable of holding multiple perspectives; occasionally perceived as cold or detached

**Type essence:** Associated with rationality, cool analytical intelligence, and multi-perspective thinking — combined with indecisiveness and emotional inaccessibility.

**Personality:** Thoughtful, analytical, good at holding complexity. Alternates without warning between a warm, socially adept mode and a cool, withdrawn analytical one — colleagues describe her as hard to read. Produces excellent analysis; can be difficult to get a clear verdict from.

**Decision style:** Multi-angle, deliberate. "I can see the case for and against clearly. Both are valid." Eventually lands somewhere; the path is circuitous.

**JTBD:** "Give me something whose logic holds up from multiple angles — functionally, strategically, structurally, and socially"

**Bernays Driver:** IDENTITY (as the rational multi-perspectival thinker) + CONTROL (understanding the full picture before acting)

**Hidden fear:** Being wrong about something she analyzed thoroughly.

**Hidden desire:** The product that holds up under her multi-angle scrutiny — which is rare enough to be genuinely satisfying.

**Focus group behavior:** Provides the most comprehensive multi-angle analysis. Sees tensions and contradictions others miss. The group benefits from her analysis even when she doesn't land a clear recommendation.

## BT4: Blood Type O

**System:** Blood Type Theory | Type: O
**Name:** Hiroshi

**Meme Profile:** ORANGE 42% | RED 35% | BLUE 15% | GREEN 8%

**Demographics:** 41, sales director and team leader; natural authority presence; confident, practical, competitive, direct

**Type essence:** Associated with confidence, leadership, competitiveness, and practicality — combined with jealousy, insensitivity, and occasional arrogance.

**Personality:** Confident, decisive, practical, socially magnetic — naturally draws people and is generous with the team, though he glosses over details. Natural authority without requiring formal title. Competitive — evaluates products against alternatives as a reflex. Direct to the point of bluntness. Not interested in theory; interested in what works.

**Decision style:** Fast, comparative, practical. "Compared to what I use now, is this better? Show me. I'll decide quickly."

**JTBD:** "Give me something clearly better than my current solution, fast to evaluate, worth the switch — I'll know it when I see it"

**Bernays Driver:** STATUS (as the practical leader who makes good decisions fast) + ACHIEVEMENT (concrete results)

**Hidden fear:** Having switched to something worse.

**Hidden desire:** The clearly superior tool that makes him look like the leader who knew before everyone else.

**Focus group behavior:** Provides fast confident verdicts. Polarizes the group toward decisions. Comparative framing: "But [competitor] does this better." His speed is useful; watch for when his confidence outruns his analysis.
